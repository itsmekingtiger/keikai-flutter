Add KkWholeWidth so that text-sized plates end on a whole pixel

A tag, a key cap, a button, a badge and the extended FAB are as wide as their text, which is never a whole number of pixels, so at pixel ratio 1 the 1px edge on the right was smeared over two pixels while the one on the left was solid. Their content now sits in KkWholeWidth, which rounds its width up by less than a pixel and keeps the content at the left, so a plate that starts on a pixel also ends on one. Two tests that pinned the padding on the right to the fraction now allow for the rounding.
